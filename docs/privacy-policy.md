# Warden Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Warden is an AI chief-of-staff assistant built by NovaPulse. You can chat with it on the web dashboard and on WhatsApp. This policy explains what data Warden handles and why.

## What we collect

- **Your WhatsApp phone number**, to identify you and reply to you.
- **The messages you send**, including text and any media you choose to send.
- **Reminders you create**, with their time and text.
- **Workspace content you add**, such as projects, backlog items and notes.

We do not collect contacts, location or payment details.

## How we use it

- To answer your messages and run the tasks you ask for.
- To deliver reminders at the time you set.
- To keep the conversation history that gives the assistant context.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising.

## Who processes it

- **Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform)** carries messages between you and Warden.
- **AI model providers** (for example Google Gemini or Meta models) process message text to generate replies, under their API terms.
- Data is stored in Warden's own database. It is not shared with anyone else.

## Retention and deletion

- We keep conversation history and reminders until you ask us to delete them.
- To delete your data, email the contact address below with your phone number. We delete it within 30 days.
- You can stop all messages at any time by blocking the number in WhatsApp.

## Security

Messages travel over encrypted connections. Webhook requests are verified with Meta's signature. Access to stored data is limited to the operator.

## Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at https://github.com/sma1692/warden/issues. Do not post personal data in the issue; we will follow up privately.
